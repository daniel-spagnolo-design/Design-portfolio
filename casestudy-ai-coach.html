The problem

Leaders and managers frequently face navigating complex interpersonal dynamics like difficult conversations, providing quality feedback, and communicating effectively. They often struggle to access timely, high-quality coaching when they need it most to enhance their performance and leadership skills. Existing resources are typically expensive or provide generic, broad guidance that isn't tailored to their specific situation or organisational context.

This leaves leaders without the information or a confidential space where they can reflect, learn, and determine contextually relevant actions to improve their performance over time. In an increasingly complex workplace, they need granular, action-oriented support on "what do I do now, and how do I do it?". Without it, they lack the clarity and confidence needed to handle challenging situations effectively, impacting their ability to make decisions and hindering their speed to action. Managers sometimes resort to inadequate, generic AI tools which pose data privacy concerns and lack grounding in relevant evidence.

TL;DR

The solution

We created an AI Coach product to help leaders and managers struggling to access timely, high-quality coaching. Coach is an AI-powered, interactive tool designed to provide real-time performance coaching. It addresses the lack of readily available, high-quality guidance by offering immediate, structured support for navigating workplace challenges, particularly difficult conversations, performance reviews, and feedback.

By leveraging insights from Culture Amp’s expert People Science and Organisational Psychology knowledge and interacting through a conversational interface, Coach helps leaders and managers to:

Gain clarity

Organising thoughts, gain insights, and get structured recommendations
